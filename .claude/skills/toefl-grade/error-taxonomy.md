# Error taxonomy

Fixed tags used in grading.json, objective items and trend analysis. Keep this list stable because trends
compare counts across attempts. Add new tags only by appending, never by renaming.

## grammar
- grammar.article: a/an/the, zero article
- grammar.tense: wrong tense or aspect, tense shifts
- grammar.agreement: subject–verb or noun–pronoun agreement
- grammar.word_order: word order, indirect questions, adverb placement
- grammar.preposition: wrong/missing preposition
- grammar.plural: countable/uncountable, plural forms
- grammar.verb_form: gerund/infinitive, participles, modals + verb form
- grammar.sentence_boundary: run-ons, comma splices, fragments
- grammar.clauses: relative/conditional/subordinate clause errors
- grammar.comparatives: comparative/superlative forms
- grammar.questions: question formation

## lexis
- lexis.word_choice: wrong word, false friend, unclear word
- lexis.collocation: unnatural combination (make/do a mistake)
- lexis.word_form: wrong part of speech (economy/economic)
- lexis.register: too informal/formal for the context
- lexis.range: repetitive or overly simple vocabulary
- lexis.spelling: spelling

## discourse
- discourse.task_completion: missed bullet, didn't answer the question
- discourse.development: ideas not explained, no example/reason
- discourse.cohesion: weak linking, unclear reference
- discourse.organization: no clear structure
- discourse.relevance: off-topic or repeats a classmate without adding
- discourse.length: too short for the task

## speaking
- speaking.fluency.pauses: long or frequent pauses
- speaking.fluency.fillers: frequent um/uh
- speaking.fluency.pace: too slow / rushed
- speaking.time_management: stopped early or cut off mid-idea
- speaking.pronunciation: words likely unclear (low recognition confidence)
- speaking.repeat_accuracy: Listen & Repeat words dropped or changed

## reading / listening (objective, assigned by the item `skill`)
reading.detail, reading.main_idea, reading.inference, reading.vocabulary, reading.vocabulary.form,
reading.purpose, reading.negative_factual, reading.cause_effect, reading.reference,
listening.detail, listening.main_idea, listening.inference, listening.purpose, listening.attitude,
listening.response, listening.organization
